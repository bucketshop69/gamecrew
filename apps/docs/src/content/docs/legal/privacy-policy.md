_Last updated: 25 July 2026_

This explains what data the GameCrew mobile app collects and why.

## What we collect

**Match activity.** Which matches you view and interact with inside the app,
so features like live commentary and Game View can work.

**Wallet address (if you connect a wallet).** If you choose to connect a
crypto wallet to use playful, on-chain features, we store the public wallet
address to associate it with your in-app activity. We never see or store
your private keys or seed phrase — wallet connections are handled by your
wallet provider, not by GameCrew.

**Device and technical data.** Standard technical information (like app
version and error logs) to help us diagnose problems and keep the app
running.

## What we don't collect

We don't require an account, email, or phone number to use GameCrew's core
features. We don't sell your data to third parties.

## Third-party services

GameCrew relies on a small number of third-party services to work:

- A sports data provider, for live match information.
- An AI provider, to generate match commentary from that data.
- Solana blockchain infrastructure, for any on-chain playful items.

These providers process the data needed to power their part of the app
(e.g. match data requests, commentary generation) under their own privacy
practices.

## Data retention

We keep match activity and technical logs only as long as needed to operate
and improve the app.

## Your choices

You can stop using GameCrew and uninstall it at any time. If you've
connected a wallet, disconnecting it in-app stops further wallet-linked
data collection going forward.

## Children

GameCrew is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect data from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be
reflected here with an updated date.
